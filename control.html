1 How Chris works

Chris is WOAH's email assistant. He works by himself every few minutes; this page lets you see what he's doing and change how he does it. No code needed.
An email arrives
Every 5 minutes Chris checks the WOAH inbox for new emails.
He works out who it's for
Using the Routing list (topics and keywords) and AI, he picks the department and the right team members from the Team list.
He forwards it
The team member gets it in their own inbox, and can answer from the Email handlers page. Outside people get a short auto-reply.
He reminds
If nobody answers, he sends reminders (CC'ing the people set in Settings). When someone answers, he tells everyone it's done.
He follows up
If WOAH emailed someone and they didn't answer, he nudges the sender to follow up.
He keeps records
He updates the Planner, the Email handlers page and the Possible Partners List, and emails the admins if something breaks.
More detail: words you'll see
  • Team list: everyone Chris can send to. If someone isn't on it, Chris never emails them, and they can't log in to these pages.
  • Routing: each department, the words that point to it, and who it goes to. "Default" is used when nothing fits.
  • Break: holidays. Nothing goes to students; everything goes to the break contacts instead.
  • Test mode: every email Chris would send goes only to the first alerts person. Good for trying changes safely.
  • Admins: the people who can open this page. Everyone else on the team only sees Email handlers and Partners.
  • Passwords: there are two (full and handlers). They're kept in Chris's script settings and are never shown on any page.
  • Changes you save here work from Chris's next check (within about 5 minutes). Every change is written in Activity with who made it.

8 For the next admin

Taking over Chris? Do these once, in this order.
  1. Get added. Ask a current admin to add you to the Team list and press Make admin next to your name (section 5). Then log in here with your own email.
  2. Get the passwords from the person handing over: the full password and the handlers password. Never post them in a chat group or a document.
  3. Put yourself on alerts. In Settings, add your email to "Who gets Chris's alerts" so you hear about problems.
  4. Update the team every term. Add new students, remove people who left (their open emails and partner contacts move to the right department automatically), and check the Routing departments still match.
  5. Check Health once a week. Green means fine. If it's red, read the alert in Activity; most problems fix themselves on the next check.
  6. Before holidays, switch the WOAH inbox and Planner to Break, and check the break contacts are right. Switch back On when term starts.
  7. Remove the old admin when they've left: take away Admin, then remove them from the team. There always has to be at least one admin.
  8. Read the WOAH Systems Guide for how the Email handlers and Partners pages work for students.
