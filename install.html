WOAH Get the app

Put the dashboard and the POS on your phone's home screen, like a normal app. Free, for iPhone and Android, no App Store needed.
← Dashboard
WOAH
The whole dashboard: Email handlers, Partners, Shop, Control and more. Install it from any dashboard page.
WOAH POS
Opens straight onto selling at the stand. Install it from the POS page. Keeps working with bad wifi.
On iPhone (use Safari)
  1. Open this site in Safari. Other browsers on iPhone can't install apps the same way.
  2. Go to the page you want as an app: the Dashboard for WOAH, or the POS for WOAH POS.
  3. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  5. Open the new icon on your home screen and log in once with the WOAH password and your own email.
On Android (use Chrome)
  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Go to the page you want as an app: the Dashboard for WOAH, or the POS for WOAH POS.
  3. Tap Install app if Chrome offers it, or tap the ⋮ menu (top right) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Open the new icon and log in once with the WOAH password and your own email.
Tip: press and hold the WOAH icon to jump straight to the POS, Email handlers or Partners.
Good to know
  • Log in with your own email. Every sale and reply is saved under the name of whoever is logged in, so don't share one phone's login.
  • It updates by itself. When the site changes, the app changes too. Nothing to reinstall.
  • No wifi at the stand? The POS still opens with the last product list, and sales are saved on the phone and sent as soon as it's back online. Pre-orders need internet.
  • Links to Gmail or Google Sheets open in their own app or the browser, then you can come back to WOAH.
