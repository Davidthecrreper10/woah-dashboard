How the shop works. Every product, colour, size, price and stock number comes from Dom's sheet (26/27 STOCK and Accounts, tab Sales Summary Report). Change a price or add a size there and the shop follows within a few minutes. New rows show up here marked New. Name new rows like Model Colour Adult/Kids (Size), e.g. Moonstone T-Shirt White Kids (M), so Chris groups them into one product. Here you only choose where each product goes (Current merch = buy now, limited by stock · Pre-order = order now, no stock limit · Not in shop), write a description and add photos. The first photo is the cover. Photos are saved in the "WOAH Shop photos" Drive folder.
