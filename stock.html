Where these numbers come from.

Start + In − Sold = End comes straight from Dom's sheet (2. Sales sum report). Change stock there, not here.

Held = sold on the Sell page but not ticked "In Dom's sheet" yet, plus website and stall orders that haven't been collected. Left = End − Held, which is what the shop and Sell page can still sell.

Below zero means more was sold than Dom's sheet says came in, so a stock-in is missing or a sale was put on the wrong size. Those need the recount. Pre-orders are people waiting for that item to arrive.
